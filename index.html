Inkfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Inkfall handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Inkfall does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

The current version of Inkfall does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or with third parties.

Future Features

We may add advertising and optional in-app purchases in a future version. Before any such feature is enabled, this policy will be updated to list the services used (for example Google AdMob or Google Play Billing) and the data they process. Payment details are always handled by Google Play; we never receive them.

How We Use Data

Data stored on your device is used only to operate the game and save your progress. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Inkfall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Game data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. Contact us for any questions regarding your data.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
