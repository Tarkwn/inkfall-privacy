Inkfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Inkfall handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Inkfall does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

The following data is collected automatically through the third-party services listed below:

  • App interactions: gameplay events such as when a run ends, how long it lasted, how many enemies were defeated, the level reached, and the map and mode played; and, for optional reward ads, when an ad was offered, started, completed or failed.
  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID and installation ID, and the device's advertising ID (used by our ad partners, see below).
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • Approximate location: country or region, estimated from your IP address or SIM.

All of this data is encrypted in transit.

Advertising

Inkfall shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward (for example double gold after a run, a revive, or a daily token). There are no forced, interstitial or banner ads.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation by these ad networks:

  • ironSource Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy

To show and measure ads, these services receive the advertising ID, device information, approximate location and ad interactions (views, clicks), and may use them to show ads that suit your interests and to prevent fraud. This data is shared with them for advertising; it is not sold by us for any other purpose.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Inkfall asks before the first ad whether ads may be personalised; you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads). Ads keep working either way, with the same rewards.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics, such as how many people are playing and how runs go, to help us improve the game.
  • Firebase Remote Config — delivers game configuration, such as update notices and which reward offers are switched on.
  • Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads — optional reward ads, as described above.

Firebase is provided by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy; LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads by Unity Technologies under the Unity privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

We may add optional in-app purchases in a future version. Before they are enabled, this policy will be updated. Payment details are always handled by Google Play; we never receive them.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used to operate the game, measure how it is played, fix problems, improve the experience, and, through our ad partners, to show the optional reward ads you choose to watch. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Inkfall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics data is processed under Google's security standards and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above (Google Analytics' default retention). Ad partners keep data under their own policies linked above.

Your Rights

You can reset the app's identifiers by clearing the app's data or reinstalling it, and your advertising ID in your device settings. Contact us for any requests regarding your data, including deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
