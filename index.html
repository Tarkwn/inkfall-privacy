Inkfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Inkfall handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Inkfall does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

The following data is collected automatically through the third-party services listed below:

  • App interactions: gameplay events such as when a run ends, how long it lasted, how many enemies were defeated, the level reached, and the map and mode played.
  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID and installation ID.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and app version.
  • Approximate location: country or region, estimated from your IP address.

We do not use the advertising ID. All of this data is encrypted in transit.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics, such as how many people are playing and how runs go, to help us improve the game.
  • Firebase Remote Config — delivers game configuration, such as update notices.

These services are provided by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy.

Future Features

We may add advertising and optional in-app purchases in a future version. Before any such feature is enabled, this policy will be updated to list the services used (for example Google AdMob or Google Play Billing) and the data they process. Payment details are always handled by Google Play; we never receive them.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to operate the game, measure how it is played, fix problems, and improve the experience. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Children's Privacy

Inkfall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics data is processed under Google's security standards and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above (Google Analytics' default retention).

Your Rights

You can reset the app's identifiers by clearing the app's data or reinstalling it. Contact us for any requests regarding your data, including deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
