Inkfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Inkfall handles user data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Inkfall does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, also in your own Google Play Games account (see Cloud Save below). We do not run servers of our own that store your progress.

The following data is collected automatically through the third-party services listed below:

  • App interactions: gameplay events such as when a run ends, how long it lasted, how many enemies were defeated, the level reached, and the map and mode played; for optional reward ads, when an ad was offered, started, completed or failed, and the estimated revenue of an ad that was shown; for the in-game store, when it was opened, which offers it showed, and when a purchase was started, completed, cancelled or failed, together with the product and the result of its receipt check; and whether cloud save sign-in and syncing worked.
  • Purchase history: which product you bought, its price and currency, and the Google Play order ID kept in your save (see In-App Purchases below).
  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app instance ID and installation ID, the device's advertising ID (used by our ad partners, see below), and, only if you play online co-op, an anonymous Unity player ID.
  • Device and diagnostic information: device model, operating system version, language, and app version, and technical performance and error data collected by the ad SDK.
  • Approximate location: country or region, estimated from your IP address or SIM.
  • Nickname (online co-op only): the name you type in the co-op menu, shown to the other players in your room.

All of this data is encrypted in transit.

Advertising

Inkfall shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward (for example double gold after a run, a revive, or a daily token). There are no forced, interstitial or banner ads.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation by these ad networks:

  • ironSource Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy

To show and measure ads, these services receive the advertising ID, device information, approximate location and ad interactions (views, clicks), and may use them to show ads that suit your interests and to prevent fraud. This data is shared with them for advertising; it is not sold by us for any other purpose.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Inkfall asks before the first ad whether ads may be personalised; you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads). Ads keep working either way, with the same rewards. Owners of the Supporter Pack collect these rewards without watching an ad.

In-App Purchases

Inkfall offers optional in-app purchases: the Supporter Pack, a one-time purchase, and token and gold packs, which can be bought more than once. Nothing in the game requires a purchase.

Purchases are processed by Google Play through Google Play Billing (the app uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to it). Your payment card, bank and billing details are entered in Google Play and handled only by Google; we never receive them.

To deliver what you bought, the game receives from Google Play the product ID, the order ID and the purchase receipt that Google signs (it contains a purchase token). The receipt is checked on your device to confirm that the purchase is genuine; it is not sent to us and not stored. The game keeps a record of delivered orders (order ID and product) in your save, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once, including after a reinstall; this record is part of the cloud save described below. When the game starts, it asks Google Play which one-time purchases you own, so the Supporter Pack is restored on a new install (or with Settings > Restore Purchases) and removed if it was refunded.

Google Play reports completed purchases (product, price and currency) to Google Analytics for Firebase so that we can see sales figures; our own store events carry the product and the result, but not order IDs or receipts.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy; you can request one from your Google Play order history.

Cloud Save

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, Inkfall stores a copy of your save in Saved Games in your own Play Games account, so that your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new device with the same Google account. The save holds your game progress, unlocks, currencies, settings, co-op nickname, Supporter Pack status and the record of delivered orders. It is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we have no access to it outside the game on your device. You can sign out of Play Games, or delete the game's saved data from your Google Play Games settings, at any time; the game then keeps your progress on this device only.

Online Co-op

When you host or join an online co-op room, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services, which creates a random player ID that is not linked to your name or Google account. Rooms are run by Unity's Multiplayer Services and game traffic between the players passes through Unity Relay servers, which see your IP address. The players in your room see your nickname, the character, weapon and colour you picked, your upgrade levels and whether you are ready or away. Room data is removed when the room closes. If you never play online co-op, none of this happens.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics, such as how many people are playing, how runs go, how reward ads and the store are used, and purchase revenue, to help us improve the game.
  • Firebase Remote Config — delivers game configuration, such as update notices, which reward offers, store and co-op features are switched on, and their limits.
  • Firebase Realtime Database — keeps the worldwide count of defeated zombies shown in the game. The game sends only the number of zombies defeated, with no identifier.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases, as described above.
  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in and Saved Games for cloud save, as described above.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer Services, Relay) — online co-op rooms, as described above.
  • Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads — optional reward ads, as described above.

Firebase, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services are provided by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy; Unity Gaming Services, LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads by Unity Technologies under the Unity privacy policy.

How We Use Data

Collected data is used to operate the game, deliver and restore purchases, check that purchases are genuine, keep your progress in cloud save, run online co-op rooms, measure how the game and its store are used, fix problems, improve the experience, and, through our ad partners, to show the optional reward ads you choose to watch. We do not sell your data.

Children's Privacy

Inkfall is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Data Security and Retention

Game data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The cloud save copy stays in your Play Games account until you delete it there. Analytics data is processed under Google's security standards and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above (Google Analytics' default retention). Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies. Co-op room data is removed when the room closes; Unity and our ad partners keep data under their own policies linked above.

Your Rights

You can reset the app's identifiers by clearing the app's data or reinstalling it, and your advertising ID in your device settings. You can delete your cloud save from your Google Play Games settings. Contact us for any requests regarding your data, including deletion.
